    About Surprizz 💝

    Making every special moment unforgettable, one surprise at a time.

    Our Story 🌟

    Surprizz was born from a simple idea — everyone deserves to feel special. We built Surprizz to help people create beautiful, heartfelt digital surprises for their loved ones without any technical knowledge.

    We are a small passionate team from India, building tools that bring smiles to people's faces.

    What We Offer 🎁

    Password-protected surprise pages with photos, music, AI messages, countdown timers, scratch cards, wish walls, voice messages — shareable with one unique link.

    Our Values 💕

    ❤️ Love First — Everything we build is centered around love.

    🔒 Privacy — Your surprises are password-protected and private.

    🌱 Accessibility — Basic features are free forever.
