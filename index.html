    About Surprizz 💝

    Making every special moment unforgettable, one surprise at a time.

    Our Story 🌟

    Surprizz was born from a simple idea — everyone deserves to feel special. We built Surprizz to help people create beautiful, heartfelt digital surprises for their loved ones without any technical knowledge. Whether it's a birthday, anniversary, or just a random day of love — Surprizz makes it magical.

    We are a small passionate team from India, building tools that bring smiles to people's faces. Our mission is to make every occasion special, memorable and personal.

    What We Offer 🎁

    Surprizz lets you create password-protected surprise pages with photos, music, AI-generated messages, countdown timers, scratch cards, wish walls, voice messages and much more — all shareable with one unique link.

    Our Values 💕

    🔒 Privacy — Your surprises are password-protected and private.

    🌱 Accessibility — Basic features are free forever for everyone.

    🚀 Innovation — We keep adding new features to make your surprises better.

    FAQ ❓

    Is Surprizz free?
    Yes! Basic features are completely free. Advanced and AI features require a small subscription.

    How do I share a surprise?
    After creating, click "Save & Get Link". Share the link and password with your loved one!

    How long is data stored?
    Your surprises are stored securely in our cloud database.

    Can I edit after creating?
    Yes! Go to "My Surprises" and click Edit anytime.

    Is payment secure?
    Yes! We use Razorpay — India's most trusted payment gateway.
